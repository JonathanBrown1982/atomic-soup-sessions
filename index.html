New to Atomic Soup?

Atomic Soup is McCann’s weekly cultural intelligence session and creative accelerator, now in its seventh year. Each week we pick one slice of culture, bring the strangest and truest things we can find, and turn them into ideas brands can actually use.

What happensA host frames the topic. Contributors each bring a truth: a short, sharp observation about how people really live, the evidence behind it, and a “what if…?” provocation. Then the room riffs.
Who it’s forEveryone, from any team, office, level or discipline. You don’t need to be a strategist. Lived experience, odd obsessions and late-night scrolling are all valid research.
What comes outTruths and ideas go into a growing library of 100+ sessions, and feed pitches, client work and the Ideas Lab. Your truth could be the start of a campaign.
This run, we’re mining the unusual. Every contributor brings one “wait, what?” exhibit: something real from the fringe that stopped you scrolling. No category reports.
